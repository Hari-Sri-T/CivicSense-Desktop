CIVICSENSE DEMONSTRATION POLICY
This is a fictional document created for demonstrations. It is not a real contract.

1. Access and use
Residents may use the community reading room from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekdays. Users must keep noise low and return borrowed materials within fourteen days.

2. Cancellation
A resident may cancel a reading-room membership by giving seven days' written notice. There is no cancellation fee. Any borrowed materials must be returned before cancellation takes effect.

3. Personal information
The reading room collects each member's name and email address to manage reservations. It does not sell personal information. Reservation records are deleted after ninety days.

4. Questions
The policy does not describe parking arrangements or weekend opening hours.
