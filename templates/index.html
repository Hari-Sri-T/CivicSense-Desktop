About CivicSense
Version 0.1.0
A local application for asking questions about civic and legal documents. Answers include explanations and the passages retrieved from your document.
How it works
  1. Extract text from a PDF, DOCX or TXT file.
  2. Index overlapping passages with MiniLM embeddings.
  3. Retrieve the five passages closest to your question.
  4. Generate an answer with Qwen2.5-3B and show the source text.
Workspace
One active document. The index and displayed conversation are held in memory and cleared when the application closes. Questions are independent; the model does not receive previous messages. Scanned PDFs need OCR before upload.
Research
The repository includes the original experiments and results, plus an evidence audit explaining their limits. Inspect the source text before relying on an answer.
